## Production function (`quarterly.csv`: `capital`, `labour_share`, `tfp_log`; `annual.csv`)

- **Capital** — perpetual inventory at average 2010 prices, million KZT. Annual real GFCF: the 2010 nominal level chained with BNS `GFCF_VOLUME_INDEX` (2000–2025). Depreciation δ = 0.0827 a year (0.0213 a quarter): the median of BNS book depreciation over gross fixed assets at the start of the year, 2000–2025 without the 2019–2021 revaluation years. Initial stock at end-1999 = I₂₀₀₀/(g+δ) with g = 0.0852 (mean real GDP growth 2000–2009): 8,550,397. From 2010-Q1 the quarterly stock accumulates BNS seasonally adjusted GFCF at 2010 prices from the end-2009 annual stock.
- Quarterly investment is BNS SA GFCF at 2010 prices benchmarked pro rata to the annual real GFCF (column `gfcf_benchmarked`): the quarterly accounts of 2010–2022 are an older vintage above the revised annual figures, and their fixed 2010 prices drift from the chain-linked annual volume index (a factor near 0.91 remains after 2023, when the nominal figures agree) (2011: annual chain 5,487,579 vs quarterly sum 6,072,629; 2012: 6,030,850 vs 6,865,529); factors by year: {2010: 0.8937, 2011: 0.9037, 2012: 0.8784, 2013: 0.8645, 2014: 0.8682, 2015: 0.8756, 2016: 0.8609, 2017: 0.8591, 2018: 0.8576, 2019: 0.9067, 2020: 0.9041, 2021: 0.8993, 2022: 0.9149, 2023: 0.913, 2024: 0.9593, 2025: 0.9108, 2026: 0.9108}. Pro rata keeps the quarterly pattern but can step at year boundaries.
- **Labour share** — compensation of employees / (GDP − net taxes on production and imports), QNA income approach, quarterly; mean 0.335. Mixed income of the self-employed sits in gross profit, so this understates labour's share.
- **TFP** — `tfp_log` = 100·(ln Y − α ln K − (1−α) ln L), rebased to 0 at its first quarter; Y = `gdp` (SA, 2010 prices), K = `capital`, L = `employment_sa`, α = 1 − mean labour share = 0.665. A residual: it absorbs utilisation, hours and measurement error, and the high α reflects the mining sector. Its change over 2010-01…2026-01 (log points ×100) by δ: δ=0.05: -21.8, δ=0.0827: -16.9, δ=0.12: -12.1. The quarterly stock depreciates investment within the year, so it runs 2–3 % below the annual end-year stock.
- Book values (`FIXED_ASSETS_*`) are historical cost with revaluations; they set δ only, never the level of K.
