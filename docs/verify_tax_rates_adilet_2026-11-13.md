## 0. How adilet is read (reusable method)

- The site is a JavaScript application over a JSON API that answers plain `curl` (no browser needed):
  `https://adilet.zan.kz/api/documents/by-ngr/<id>?language=rus` -> current edition: `document.text_content` (HTML), plus the
  «Информация» card (`publication_reference` = official publications with dates, `adoption_date`, `status`, …);
  `https://adilet.zan.kz/api/documents/<uuid>/versions?page=1&page_size=500` -> the «История изменений» list: one row per edition
  with `document_id` (uuid of the edition) and `revision_date` (UTC; add 6 h to get the calendar date shown on the site);
  `https://adilet.zan.kz/api/documents/<edition uuid>` -> full text of that archived edition.
- In a browser the same things are: `https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/<id>` (text), `…/<id>?tab=publication` («Информация»),
  `…/<id>?tab=history` («История изменений»), and an archived edition is `https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/<edition uuid>`
  (the page says «Вы просматриваете редакцию dd.mm.yyyy»). The old pattern `/rus/archive/docs/<id>/<dd.mm.yyyy>` returns the empty shell on adilet.zan.kz, but still works with plain curl on the old site kept online: `https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/archive/docs/<id>/<dd.mm.yyyy>` (also `…/docs/<id>/history`, `…/docs/<id>/info`); checked on P080000328_ / 08.04.2008. This report used the new site's API.
- Caveat: an edition is dated by the day of the AMENDING ACT, not by the day the amendment takes effect, and it already contains
  text that enters into force later (e.g. the 2001 Code edition of 11.12.2006 already shows VAT 14 and the 2008 social-tax scale).
  The month of a step must therefore be taken from the commencement clause / footnote, not from the edition date.
  `publication_date` in the API is the date of entry in the database, not of official publication — use `publication_reference`.
- Headless Chromium also works (first page load of a session shows an error + a welcome dialog; reload, close the dialog
  «Продолжить в тестовой версии», use a viewport >= 1500 px or the tab labels are hidden), but it is not needed.

## 4. What could not be checked on adilet, and why

- **OIL_EXPORT_DUTY_RATE 2008-10 (month)**: PP 29.08.2008 № 805 enters into force 30 days after first official publication. The «Информация»
  card lists «САПП Республики Казахстан, 2008 г., N 37, ст. 401» (no date) and «"Официальная газета" от 18 октября 2008 года N 42 (408)».
  adilet gives no date for the САПП issue, so 11 October 2008 cannot be derived there. If САПП № 37 came out about 10–11 September 2008
  the press date is right (203,8 from October); if the first publication were the newspaper of 18.10.2008, the start would be 17–18.11.2008.
  The value 203,8 itself is confirmed.
- **Texts of 1995**: adilet has no edition of Law (Decree) 24.04.1995 № 2235 earlier than 27.09.1996, so VAT 20 % and CIT 30 % for
  July 1995 – September 1996 are confirmed only through that edition (no amendment footnote on art. 66; the only footnote on art. 30 concerns its п. 2)
  and through art. 179 («вводится в действие с 1 июля 1995 года»). Publication card: «Ведомости Верховного Совета РК, 1995 г., N 6, ст.43» only.
- **Publication dates of САПП issues** in general are not given on adilet (328, 805); for acts of 2022 and 2025 the card gives the date of
  the electronic «Эталонный контрольный банк» publication (25.03.2022, 30.04.2025), which is the first official publication.
- Not every archived edition was opened (the 2008 Code has 158, the 2017 Code 78). For each step the amending law and one edition on
  each side of the step were read; continuity inside long constant stretches (e.g. VAT 12 % 2009–2025, copper 5,7 % 2009–2022) rests on the
  amendment footnotes of the article plus yearly editions (2008 Code: 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017; 2017 Code: 2017, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025).
- P1100001739 (PP 31.12.2011 № 1739) was downloaded but not needed for any key (crude oil stayed 40 until PP № 321).
- Amendments of 2026 to PP № 1049 (editions 14.07, 27.08, 11.09.2026) were downloaded but not analysed — they do not bear on any yaml key (gap from 2025-05).
